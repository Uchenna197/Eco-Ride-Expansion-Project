
# Risk Register – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Purpose
Identify, assess, and manage risks that could impact the success of Eco-Ride implementation.

---

## 2. Risk Table

| ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|----|----------------|-----------|-------|-----------|-------|
| R-01 | Delay in requirements sign-off | Medium | High | Schedule buffer & early stakeholder engagement | BA / PM |
| R-02 | Integration failure with Payment Gateway | Low | High | Early API testing, fallback plan | TL / DEV |
| R-03 | Low user adoption | Medium | Medium | User awareness campaigns, pilot testing | OPS / PM |
| R-04 | Regulatory changes | Low | High | Compliance review & legal monitoring | CL / PM |
| R-05 | Technical performance issues | Medium | Medium | Load testing & optimization | QA / TL |
| R-06 | Resource unavailability | Medium | Medium | Cross-training & backup resources | PM |

---

## 3. Risk Monitoring
- Risks reviewed weekly in status meetings
- New risks added as identified
- Risk status updated and mitigation tracked
