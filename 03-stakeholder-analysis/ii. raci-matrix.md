
# RACI Matrix – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Purpose
This document defines **roles and responsibilities** for key project activities using a **RACI model**:
- **R** – Responsible (does the work)
- **A** – Accountable (owns the outcome)
- **C** – Consulted (provides input)
- **I** – Informed (kept up to date)

The RACI matrix helps avoid role confusion, duplication of effort, and accountability gaps during the Eco-Ride project.

---

## 2. Roles Definition

| Role | Description |
|-----|------------|
| Product Owner (PO) | Owns product vision and priorities |
| Project Manager (PM) | Manages delivery, schedule, and risks |
| Business Analyst (BA) | Gathers, analyses, and documents requirements |
| Technical Lead (TL) | Oversees technical design and architecture |
| Development Team (DEV) | Builds the solution |
| QA / Test Team (QA) | Tests solution quality |
| Operations Manager (OPS) | Oversees operational readiness |
| Compliance / Legal (CL) | Ensures regulatory compliance |
| Riders & Drivers (USERS) | End users of the system |

---

| Deployment | I | R | I | A | R | C | C | I | I |
| Operational Readiness | I | C | I | C | I | I | A/R | I | I |
| Compliance Review | I | I | C | C | I | I | I | A/R | I |
| Change Management | A | R | C | C | I | I | C | I | I |
| Project Closure | A | R | C | C | I | I | I | I | I |

---

## 4. Key Observations
- The **Business Analyst** is primarily **Responsible** for requirements-related activities.
- The **Product Owner** is **Accountable** for scope and approvals.
- The **Project Manager** owns delivery and coordination.
- Compliance is consulted or accountable where regulatory concerns apply.
- End users are consulted mainly during elicitation and UAT.

---

## 5. Usage Guidelines
- Only **one “A” (Accountable)** is assigned per activity.
- Multiple “R” roles are allowed where collaboration is required.
- The RACI matrix should be reviewed if scope or team structure changes.
