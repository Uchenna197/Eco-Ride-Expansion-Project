
# To-Be Process – Eco-Ride

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the **future state (To-Be) process** enabled by the Eco-Ride platform.

The goal is to improve:
- User experience
- Operational efficiency
- Transparency and safety

---

## 2. Actors
- Rider
- Driver
- Eco-Ride System
- Payment Gateway
- Admin

---

## 3. Future Process Flow (To-Be)

1. Rider logs into Eco-Ride app
2. Rider enters pickup and destination
3. System displays nearby available vehicles
4. Rider books a ride
5. Driver accepts the request
6. Rider tracks the ride in real time
7. Payment is processed digitally
8. Ride is completed
9. Rider rates the experience

---

## 4. Improvements Over As-Is

| Area | Improvement |
|----|------------|
| Booking | App-based real-time availability |
| Pricing | Transparent fare calculation |
| Safety | Verified users and drivers |
| Payments | Secure digital payments |
| Tracking | GPS-based ride tracking |
| Feedback | Rating and review system |

---

## 5. Business Benefits
- Improved customer satisfaction
- Better operational control
- Increased trust and adoption
- Data-driven decision-making
