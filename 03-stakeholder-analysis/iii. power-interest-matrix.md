
# Power–Interest Matrix – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Purpose
This document categorizes Eco-Ride stakeholders based on their **level of power** and **level of interest** in the project.

The Power–Interest Matrix helps determine:
- How closely each stakeholder should be managed
- The appropriate engagement and communication strategy
- Where to focus stakeholder management effort

---

## 2. Power–Interest Grid Overview

Stakeholders are grouped into four categories:

1. **High Power / High Interest** – Manage Closely  
2. **High Power / Low Interest** – Keep Satisfied  
3. **Low Power / High Interest** – Keep Informed  
4. **Low Power / Low Interest** – Monitor  

---

## 3. Stakeholder Classification

### 3.1 High Power / High Interest (Manage Closely)

| Stakeholder | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| Product Owner | Owns product vision and priorities |
| Project Manager | Accountable for delivery and success |
| Technical Lead | Controls technical decisions and feasibility |

**Engagement Strategy:**  
- Frequent involvement  
- Decision-making authority  
- Regular reviews and workshops  

---

### 3.2 High Power / Low Interest (Keep Satisfied)

| Stakeholder | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| Compliance / Legal | Influences approval but not day-to-day work |
| Senior Management | Strategic oversight, limited operational involvement |

**Engagement Strategy:**  
- High-level updates  
- Escalate only key risks or decisions  
- Avoid unnecessary detail  

---

### 3.3 Low Power / High Interest (Keep Informed)

| Stakeholder | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| Riders (End Users) | Directly impacted by solution |
| Drivers | Operational users of the system |
| Operations Team | Responsible for day-to-day usage |

**Engagement Strategy:**  
- Regular updates  
- Feedback sessions  
- Surveys and UAT involvement  

---

### 3.4 Low Power / Low Interest (Monitor)

| Stakeholder | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| External Service Providers | Limited influence and involvement |
| Third-party Vendors | Used as supporting services |

**Engagement Strategy:**  
- Minimal communication  
- Engage only when required  

---

## 4. Usage Notes
- Stakeholder positions may change during the project lifecycle
- The matrix should be reviewed at major milestones
- Changes should be reflected in the communication and engagement plans
