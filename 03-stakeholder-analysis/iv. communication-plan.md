
# Stakeholder Communication Plan – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Purpose
This document defines **how, when, and what** information will be communicated to stakeholders throughout the Eco-Ride project.

The objective is to ensure:
- Clear, timely, and consistent communication
- Stakeholder alignment and transparency
- Reduced risk of misunderstandings or missed expectations

---

## 2. Communication Principles
- Communication will be clear, concise, and audience-appropriate
- Sensitive information will be shared on a need-to-know basis
- Feedback will be encouraged and documented
- Decisions and approvals will be recorded

---

## 3. Communication Matrix

| Stakeholder | Information Shared | Channel | Frequency | Owner |
|------------|-------------------|---------|-----------|-------|
| Product Owner | Progress, risks, approvals | Meetings / Email | Weekly | PM |
| Project Manager | Status updates, issues | Meetings | Weekly | BA |
| Technical Lead | Requirements, design decisions | Workshops / Chat | As needed | BA |
| Development Team | Functional requirements | Documentation / Stand-ups | Ongoing | BA |
| QA Team | Acceptance criteria, changes | Email / Meetings | As needed | BA |
| Operations Manager | Operational impacts | Meetings | Bi-weekly | PM |
| Compliance / Legal | Regulatory requirements | Formal Documents | As required | PM |
| Riders | Feature updates | App Notifications / Surveys | Periodic | OPS |
| Drivers | Process updates | App Notifications / SMS | Periodic | OPS |

---

## 4. Key Communication Events

| Event | Audience | Purpose |
|-----|---------|---------|
| Project Kick-off | Core team | Align on scope and objectives |
| Requirements Workshops | Stakeholders | Elicit and validate requirements |
| Design Review | PO, TL | Validate solution design |
| UAT Sessions | Users | Validate solution against needs |
| Go-Live Announcement | All stakeholders | Confirm deployment |

---

## 5. Escalation Path
- Issues are first raised with the Project Manager
- High-impact risks are escalated to the Product Owner
- Regulatory issues are escalated to Compliance / Legal

---

## 6. Review & Maintenance
- This plan will be reviewed at each major milestone
- Updates will be made if stakeholders or communication needs change
