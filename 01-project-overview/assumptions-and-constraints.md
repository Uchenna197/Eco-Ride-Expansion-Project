
# Assumptions & Constraints – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Overview
This document outlines the key assumptions and constraints identified for the Eco-Ride project. These factors influence planning, delivery, and risk management and are reviewed throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## 2. Assumptions
The following assumptions are considered true for the purposes of this project:

1. Target users own smartphones and have reliable internet access.
2. Digital payment methods (cards, wallets, mobile payments) are widely adopted by users.
3. Required regulatory approvals and permits will be obtained before pilot launch.
4. GPS and mobile network coverage are sufficient in the operational areas.
5. Fleet maintenance and operational support will be available throughout the project.
6. Stakeholders will be available for requirements elicitation, reviews, and approvals.
7. Initial demand will be concentrated in high-traffic urban zones.

---

## 3. Constraints
The project is subject to the following constraints:

### 3.1 Budget Constraints
- Limited funding may restrict the initial number of bikes and scooters.
- Cost limitations may affect the scope of marketing and promotional activities.

### 3.2 Time Constraints
- The project must meet a defined pilot launch deadline.
- Regulatory timelines may impact overall delivery schedules.

### 3.3 Regulatory & Compliance Constraints
- Compliance with local transport, safety, and environmental regulations is mandatory.
- Data protection and user privacy requirements must be adhered to.

### 3.4 Technical Constraints
- Dependence on third-party services (payment gateways, GPS providers).
- System performance is influenced by network availability and device compatibility.

### 3.5 Operational Constraints
- Limited operational staff during the pilot phase.
- Maintenance capacity may affect fleet availability.

---

## 4. Impact on Project Planning
- Assumptions will be validated during early project phases.
- Constraints will be managed through scope prioritization and risk mitigation.
- Any changes to assumptions or constraints will be logged and reviewed as part of change management.
