# Project Objectives & KPIs – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Overview
This document defines the business objectives of the Eco-Ride project and the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used to measure success. The KPIs ensure that the solution delivers measurable value and aligns with strategic goals.

---

## 2. Business Objectives
The Eco-Ride project aims to:

1. Reduce traffic congestion in targeted urban areas
2. Lower carbon emissions from short-distance travel
3. Provide affordable, reliable, and convenient mobility options
4. Achieve sustainable revenue through ride usage
5. Improve efficiency in urban transportation management

---

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 3.1 User Adoption & Usage
| KPI | Description |
|----|------------|
| Number of registered users | Total users registered on the platform |
| Daily active riders | Number of users completing at least one ride per day |
| Ride completion rate | Percentage of rides successfully completed |
| Average rides per user | Frequency of usage per user |

---

### 3.2 Financial Performance
| KPI | Description |
|----|------------|
| Revenue per ride | Average income generated per completed ride |
| Monthly ride revenue | Total monthly revenue from rides |
| Cost per ride | Average operational cost per ride |

---

### 3.3 Operational Efficiency
| KPI | Description |
|----|------------|
| Vehicle availability rate | Percentage of fleet available for use |
| Average downtime per vehicle | Time vehicles are unavailable due to maintenance |
| Incident rate | Number of reported safety or operational incidents |

---

### 3.4 Environmental Impact
| KPI | Description |
|----|------------|
| Reduction in short-distance car trips | Estimated decrease in car usage |
| Estimated CO₂ emissions reduced | Carbon emissions avoided through Eco-Ride usage |

---

## 4. Success Criteria
The Eco-Ride project will be considered successful if:
- User adoption shows consistent month-on-month growth
- Ride completion rate remains high with minimal failures
- Revenue targets are met or exceeded
- Fleet availability remains above acceptable operational thresholds
- Measurable reduction in emissions is observed in pilot areas

---

## 5. KPI Review & Reporting
- KPIs will be reviewed monthly during the pilot phase
- Reports will be generated via the admin dashboard
- Insights will be used to drive operational and product improvements

---

## 6. Alignment to Business Goals
These objectives and KPIs ensure the Eco-Ride solution delivers:
- Business value
- Operational efficiency
- Environmental sustainability
- Data-driven decision-making
