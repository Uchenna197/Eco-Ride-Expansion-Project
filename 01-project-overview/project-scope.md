# Project Scope – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Project Purpose
The purpose of the Eco-Ride project is to provide a sustainable, technology-enabled solution for short-distance urban transport. The solution will reduce traffic congestion, lower emissions, and provide residents with an affordable and convenient mobility option.

---

## 2. In-Scope
The following deliverables and features are included within the project boundaries:

### 2.1 User-Facing Features
- Mobile app for users to:
  - Register and verify identity
  - Locate available bikes and scooters
  - Book and start rides
  - Make digital payments (wallet, card, mobile payment)

### 2.2 Admin Features
- Fleet management dashboard
- Ride tracking and reporting
- User management and support
- Analytics and basic operational reporting

### 2.3 Operational Scope
- GPS tracking of vehicles
- Docking stations for bikes and scooters
- Integration with payment service providers

### 2.4 Project Management & BA Deliverables
- Requirements elicitation and documentation
- Stakeholder engagement and analysis
- Solution design and process flows
- UAT planning and support
- Post-implementation review

---

## 3. Out-of-Scope
The following items are explicitly excluded from this project:

- Manufacturing of bikes or scooters
- Long-distance or inter-city transportation
- Physical infrastructure construction beyond docking stations
- Non-digital or cash-only payment methods
- Third-party app integration unrelated to Eco-Ride operations

---

## 4. Boundaries & Constraints
- Project timeline is limited to a defined pilot launch period
- Budget constraints may limit the initial fleet size
- Compliance with city transport and safety regulations is required
- Technology limitations (e.g., GPS, network coverage) may affect service availability

---

## 5. Assumptions
- Target users possess smartphones with internet access
- Digital payment adoption is sufficient
- Regulatory approvals are obtained before launch
- Vehicle maintenance will be handled according to the operational plan
