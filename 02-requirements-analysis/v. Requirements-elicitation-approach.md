
# Requirements Elicitation Approach – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Purpose
This document outlines the structured approach used to **identify, gather, validate, and prioritize requirements** for the Eco-Ride project.

The goal of the elicitation process is to ensure that business needs, stakeholder expectations, and solution constraints are clearly understood before solution design and implementation.

---

## 2. Elicitation Objectives
- Understand the business problem Eco-Ride aims to solve
- Identify stakeholder needs and pain points
- Gather functional and non-functional requirements
- Validate requirements for clarity, completeness, and feasibility
- Reduce rework and misalignment during later project phases

---

## 3. Key Stakeholders Involved

| Stakeholder Group | Role in Elicitation |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Product Owner | Defines business goals and priorities |
| Riders (Users) | Provide user needs and experience expectations |
| Drivers | Provide operational and usability insights |
| Operations Team | Defines operational workflows and constraints |
| Technical Team | Advises on feasibility and system constraints |
| Compliance / Legal | Ensures regulatory and data privacy compliance |

---

## 4. Elicitation Techniques Used

### 4.1 Stakeholder Interviews
- One-on-one interviews with key stakeholders
- Used to understand goals, pain points, and expectations
- Effective for capturing detailed qualitative insights

---

### 4.2 Workshops
- Facilitated group sessions involving multiple stakeholders
- Used to align understanding, resolve conflicts, and prioritize requirements
- Encouraged collaboration and shared ownership

---

### 4.3 Observation (Shadowing)
- Observed how drivers and riders interact with existing transport solutions
- Identified usability issues and process gaps
- Helped validate assumptions made during interviews

---

### 4.4 Document Analysis
- Reviewed existing transport policies, regulatory documents, and competitor platforms
- Helped identify mandatory requirements and industry standards

---

### 4.5 Surveys & Questionnaires
- Distributed surveys to potential users
- Collected feedback on desired features, pricing, and service expectations
- Useful for reaching a large audience efficiently

---

## 5. Elicitation Process

1. Identify and categorize stakeholders  
2. Plan elicitation sessions and select techniques  
3. Conduct elicitation activities  
4. Document findings and initial requirements  
5. Validate requirements with stakeholders  
6. Resolve conflicts and gaps  
7. Prioritize requirements for delivery  

---

## 6. Requirement Types Captured

| Requirement Type | Description |
|------------------|------------|
| Business Requirements | High-level business goals and objectives |
| Stakeholder Requirements | Needs and expectations of users and internal teams |
| Functional Requirements | System behaviors and features |
| Non-Functional Requirements | Performance, security, usability, and quality attributes |
| Regulatory Requirements | Legal and compliance obligations |

---

## 7. Validation Approach
- Requirements reviewed with stakeholders for accuracy
- Walkthrough sessions conducted to confirm understanding
- Ambiguities resolved before final approval
- Changes logged through a controlled change process

---

## 8. Challenges & Mitigation

| Challenge | Mitigation |
|---------|-----------|
| Conflicting stakeholder priorities | Facilitated prioritization workshops |
| Vague or unclear requirements | Used probing questions and examples |
| Time constraints | Focused on high-impact requirements first |
| Changing business needs | Applied change control procedures |

---
