
# Stakeholder Register – Eco-Ride Project

## 1. Purpose
This document identifies and classifies all stakeholders involved in the Eco-Ride project.  
It helps ensure that stakeholder expectations, influence, and communication needs are clearly understood and managed throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Identification

| ID | Stakeholder | Role / Title | Organisation | Interest Level | Influence Level |
|----|------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|-----------------|
| ST-01 | Product Owner | Product Strategy & Vision | Eco-Ride | High | High |
| ST-02 | Project Manager | Project Delivery | Eco-Ride | High | High |
| ST-03 | Business Analyst | Requirements & Analysis | Eco-Ride | High | Medium |
| ST-04 | Riders (Users) | End Users | External | High | Medium |
| ST-05 | Drivers | Service Providers | External | High | Medium |
| ST-06 | Operations Manager | Daily Operations | Eco-Ride | Medium | High |
| ST-07 | Technical Lead | System Architecture | Eco-Ride | High | High |
| ST-08 | Development Team | Solution Implementation | Eco-Ride | Medium | Medium |
| ST-09 | QA / Test Team | Testing & Quality Assurance | Eco-Ride | Medium | Medium |
| ST-10 | Compliance / Legal | Regulatory Oversight | External | Medium | High |
| ST-11 | Payment Gateway Provider | Payment Processing | External | Medium | Medium |
| ST-12 | GPS / Map Service Provider | Location Services | External | Low | Low |

---

## 3. Stakeholder Roles & Expectations

| Stakeholder | Key Expectations |
|------------|------------------|
| Product Owner | Solution aligns with business goals and market needs |
| Project Manager | Timely delivery within scope and budget |
| Business Analyst | Clear, validated, and traceable requirements |
| Riders | Easy booking, reliability, safety, and affordability |
| Drivers | Fair ride allocation, clear earnings visibility |
| Operations Manager | Smooth day-to-day operations |
| Technical Lead | Feasible and scalable solution design |
| QA Team | Testable and clearly defined requirements |
| Compliance | Regulatory and data protection compliance |

---

## 4. Power–Interest Classification

### High Power / High Interest
- Product Owner  
- Project Manager  
- Technical Lead  

**Management Strategy:**  
> Manage closely and involve in key decisions.

---

### High Power / Low Interest
- Compliance / Legal  

**Management Strategy:**  
> Keep satisfied and consult when required.

---

### Low Power / High Interest
- Riders  
- Drivers  

**Management Strategy:**  
> Keep informed and actively gather feedback.

---

### Low Power / Low Interest
- External Service Providers  

**Management Strategy:**  
> Monitor with minimal effort.

---

## 5. Communication Preferences

| Stakeholder | Preferred Channel | Frequency |
|------------|------------------|-----------|
| Product Owner | Meetings / Email | Weekly |
| Project Manager | Meetings / Reports | Weekly |
| Technical Lead | Workshops / Slack | As needed |
| Riders | Surveys / App Notifications | Periodic |
| Drivers | App Notifications / SMS | Periodic |
| Operations Manager | Meetings | Bi-weekly |
| Compliance | Formal Documents | As required |

---

## 6. Risks Related to Stakeholders

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|----|--------|------------|
| Conflicting stakeholder priorities | Delays, rework | Early alignment workshops |
| Low user engagement | Poor adoption | Regular feedback sessions |
| Regulatory changes | Compliance risk | Continuous legal review |
| External vendor dependency | Delivery delays | Clear SLAs and backups |

---

## 7. Review & Maintenance
- The stakeholder register will be reviewed at major project milestones.
- New stakeholders will be added as identified.
- Changes will be communicated to the project team.
