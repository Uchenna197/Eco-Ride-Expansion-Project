
# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## Eco-Ride Project

---

## 1. Background

Urban areas are experiencing increasing traffic congestion, rising transportation costs, and growing environmental concerns due to carbon emissions. Short-distance travel within cities is often inefficient, expensive, or environmentally harmful.

Eco-Ride is a proposed sustainable urban mobility solution that provides electric bikes and scooters supported by a mobile application. The solution aims to offer an affordable, eco-friendly, and convenient alternative for short-distance travel.

---

## 2. Business Problem Statement

City residents lack access to a reliable, affordable, and environmentally sustainable mode of transport for short-distance travel. Existing options contribute to congestion, pollution, and increased commute times.

Without intervention, these challenges will continue to negatively impact quality of life, economic productivity, and environmental sustainability.

---

## 3. Business Objectives

The primary objectives of the Eco-Ride project are to:

- Reduce traffic congestion in urban areas
- Lower carbon emissions associated with short-distance travel
- Provide affordable and convenient mobility options for users
- Generate sustainable revenue through ride usage
- Improve urban transportation efficiency through technology

---

## 4. Scope Definition

### 4.1 In-Scope

- Mobile application for users to:
  - Register and authenticate
  - Locate available bikes/scooters
  - Book and start rides
  - Make digital payments
- Admin dashboard for:
  - Fleet monitoring and management
  - Ride tracking and reporting
  - User management
- GPS-based tracking of rides
- Digital payment integration
- User support and basic safety features

### 4.2 Out-of-Scope

- Manufacturing of bikes or scooters
- Long-distance or inter-city transportation
- Physical infrastructure construction beyond docking stations
- Manual (non-digital) payment methods

---

## 6. High-Level Business Requirements

| ID | Business Requirement |
|----|----------------------|
| BR-01 | Users must be able to register and verify their identity |
| BR-02 | Users must be able to locate nearby available vehicles |
| BR-03 | Users must be able to book and start a ride digitally |
| BR-04 | The system must calculate ride cost automatically |
| BR-05 | Users must be able to pay electronically |
| BR-06 | Admin users must monitor fleet usage and availability |
| BR-07 | The system must provide basic usage and performance reports |

---

## 7. Assumptions

- Users possess smartphones with internet access
- Digital payment adoption is sufficiently high in the target city
- Regulatory approvals will be granted before launch
- GPS coverage is reliable in operational areas

---

## 8. Constraints

- Budget limitations may restrict initial fleet size
- Compliance with local transport and safety regulations
- Project timeline constrained by pilot launch deadlines

---

## 9. Risks and Mitigation (High-Level)

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|-----|--------|------------|
| Low user adoption | Reduced ROI | Pilot launch and awareness campaigns |
| Payment system failure | User dissatisfaction | Multiple payment gateways |
| Vandalism or theft | Operational losses | GPS tracking and penalties |
| Regulatory delays | Project delays | Early stakeholder engagement |

---

## 10. Success Metrics (KPIs)

- Number of registered users
- Daily active riders
- Ride completion rate
- Average revenue per ride
- Reduction in short-distance car usage

---

## 11. Dependencies

- Completion of mobile and backend development
- Integration with payment service providers
- Approval from city regulatory bodies
- Availability of operational fleet
