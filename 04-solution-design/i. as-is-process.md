
# As-Is Process – Eco-Ride

## 1. Purpose
This document describes the **current state (As-Is) process** for urban ride services before the Eco-Ride solution is introduced.

The objective is to identify:
- Existing workflows
- Pain points
- Inefficiencies and risks

---

## 2. Actors
- Rider
- Driver
- Traditional Transport Operator

---

## 3. Current Process Flow (As-Is)

1. Rider searches manually for available transport
2. Rider negotiates price verbally
3. Driver confirms availability
4. Rider pays with cash or informal transfer
5. Ride details are not tracked digitally
6. No formal feedback or rating system exists

---

## 4. Pain Points

| Area | Issue |
|----|------|
| Availability | Difficulty finding nearby rides |
| Pricing | Inconsistent and non-transparent fares |
| Safety | No rider or driver verification |
| Tracking | No real-time ride tracking |
| Payments | Limited payment options |
| Feedback | No structured rating system |

---

## 5. Business Impact
- Poor customer experience
- Limited operational visibility
- Low trust between riders and drivers
- Revenue leakage
